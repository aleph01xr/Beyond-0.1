# Guardian Character Design & Encounter Specifications

## Floor 6: Elyndor, Keeper of the Last Hymn
- **Aesthetic**: Sacred, radiant holy choir angel / ancient sanctuary precentor.
- **Lore**: An ancient precentor of the holy sanctuary who swore an eternal oath to guard the last sacred hymns and prayers when the sanctuary fell silent. Clad in consecrated ivory and radiant gold choir vestments, Elyndor preserves the holy songs through their divine golden harp.
- **Visuals**:
  - **Sacred Celestial Golden Harp**: Held and played gracefully in Elyndor's hands. Features a sculpted angelic-wing golden pillar (`#F59E0B`, `#FBBF24`), an arched neck with golden tuning pegs, a consecrated alabaster soundboard with gold rosettes, and 5 luminous vibrating strings (`#FEF08A`, `#FFFBEB`) with strumming finger animations.
  - **Holy Floating Musical Notes**: Golden sacred music notes (`♪`, `♫`, `♩`) drift upward in an arcing melody with holy starlight motes.
  - **Holy Angelic Visage & Hair**: Serene, noble visage with luminous platinum-gold hair (`#FEF08A`, `#FDE68A`, `#FFFBEB`), warm glowing amber-gold eyes (`#D97706`, `#FEF08A`), and a radiant 7-ray solar-angelic aureole / sunburst crown.
  - **Consecrated Liturgical Robes**: Pristine sacred alabaster-ivory choir vestments (`#FAF8F5`, `#F3EFE6`) with a flowing golden sun-cross stole (`#F59E0B`, `#FEF08A`) and gold-hemmed fringe.
  - **Seraphic Angel Wings**: Wide, feathered seraphic choir wings in warm celestial pearl-white/ivory (`rgba(255, 253, 245, 0.96)`) with luminous gold quill spines (`#FBBF24`, `#FDE047`).
- **Abilities (4 Unique)**:
  1. *Sacred Harp Resonance*: Expanding concentric harmonic acoustic shockwave ring from the harp.
  2. *Psalm of the Forgotten*: 3 targeted ground prayer sigils that detonate into holy starlight.
  3. *Harp Arpeggio Chord*: 5-note sweeping celestial chord volley fired in an arc.
  4. *Chorus of Sacred Hymns*: Full-arena 360° psalm starburst summoning holy choir minions.

## Floor 7: Zadkiel, Keeper of the Ashen Reliquary
- **Aesthetic**: Reverent, ancient armored reliquary angel.
- **Lore**: The warden who sacrificed their life to defend the vault of departed saints and lost names. Clad in tarnished silver and gold plate, their spirit animates ancestral armor.
- **Visuals**: Imposing humanoid knight, silver-steel plate with gold inlays, smoking incense censer, and dual heavy silver-steel reliquary wings (`rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.95)`) tipped with antique gold relic quills (`#D97706`).
- **Abilities (4 Unique)**:
  1. *Relic Censer Smite*: Heavy forward censer slam with 4 radial ash shockwaves.
  2. *Ancestral Relic Blade Barrage*: 8 sacred relic blade projectiles launched in a wide cone.
  3. *Memorial Monolith Drop*: Calls down 2 consecrated memorial pillars creating lingering holy zones.
  4. *Judgment of the Departed*: 3-beam laser sweep from the Eye of Remembrance.

## Floor 8: Sariel, Warden of the Veiled Scripture
- **Aesthetic**: Mysterious, celestial-void priestess angel.
- **Lore**: A high priestess and archivist who sought forbidden truths, bound to the chapel where holy scriptures clash with corrupted void energy.
- **Visuals**: Floating humanoid form shrouded in starry veil vestments, floating astrolabe, and 4 celestial-void wings blending astral violet plumage (`rgba(147, 51, 234, 0.9)`) and deep forbidden void feathers (`rgba(88, 28, 135, 0.82)`) with starlight tips.
- **Abilities (4 Unique)**:
  1. *Forbidden Scripture Seals*: 4 corrupted scripture seals placed on the arena floor that detonate into void fissures.
  2. *Veiled Starlight Step*: Blinks across the chamber, leaving an astral warp detonation at the departure point.
  3. *Astrolabe Singularity*: Gravity vortex pulling nearby adventurers inward while emitting void pulses.
  4. *Heretical Scripture Volley*: 8-way rotating spiral of corrupted scripture pages.

## Floor 9: Seraphel, Sentinel of the Final Seal
- **Aesthetic**: Radiant, imposing, solar-divine archangel.
- **Lore**: The penultimate line of defense guarding the passage to the Warden's Gate, infused with overwhelming celestial light.
- **Visuals**: Blinding radiant aura, white and gold plate armor, luminous solar lance, and 4 majestic feathered starlight ivory wings (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`) with solar-gold quill borders (`#FDE047`).
- **Abilities (4 Unique)**:
  1. *Final Seal Judgement Pillars*: 3 celestial pillars summoned beneath adventurers that erupt after 1s.
  2. *Starlight Spear Ray*: Piercing celestial beam fired directly along the lance's axis.
  3. *Seraphic Wing Gale*: 9-feather golden feather fan with explosive radial knockback.
  4. *Celestial Nave Supernova*: Delayed arena-wide starlight detonation with expanding outer rings.

## Floor 10: Oriphiel, The Last Warden
- **Aesthetic**: Sacred Divine Solar Archangel / Supreme Warden of Light & Absolute Protection.
- **Lore**: The supreme guardian of the sanctuary entrusted with safeguarding the innermost boundary and the monumental Warden's Gate. Fights with unyielding fidelity to ancient divine law, standing before the sacred gate of protection. Defeating Oriphiel unseals the gate into the deeper sanctuary.
- **Visuals**:
  - **Pure White & Gold Armor**: Consecrated white alabaster and solar-gold plate cuirass (`#FAF8F5`, `#E5DDD0`, `#F59E0B`, `#FEF08A`) with an engraved solar protection cross on the breastplate.
  - **Open Solar-Crown Helm & Visage**: White alabaster helm adorned with a radiant 9-ray solar sunburst crown (`#FEF08A`, `#F59E0B`), serene amber-gold eyes of divine judgment (`#D97706`, `#FEF08A`), and flowing platinum-gold hair.
  - **4 Monumental Archangel Wings**: Four colossal wings of pure celestial ivory feathering (`rgba(255, 253, 245, 0.96)`) bordered with luminous solar-gold quill spines (`#F59E0B`, `#FDE047`).
  - **Royal Sanctuary Aegis Cape**: Billowing white-and-gold sanctuary cape embroidered with sacred golden sunburst crosses (`#FAF8F5`, `#F59E0B`).
  - **Greatblade of Consecrated Aegis**: Two-handed broadsword of radiant white steel with a golden solar fuller and glowing holy runes.
  - **Sacred Solar Aegis Shield**: Radiant golden sunburst barrier shield (`rgba(254, 240, 138, 0.95)`, `#F59E0B`) with an inlaid solar cross displayed during windups and charges.
- **Abilities (4 Unique)**:
  1. *Sanctuary Gate Collapse*: Oriphiel leaps skyward and crashes down before the Warden's Gate, generating radial shockwaves.
  2. *Warden's Judgement Crescent*: 180° sweep with the Greatblade of Consecrated Aegis, firing 7 golden energy crescents.
  3. *Warden's Binding Sigils*: 4 perimeter warding sigils that generate intersecting starlight barrier beams.
  4. *Rune of the Last Warden*: Dual clockwise and counterclockwise starlight spiral barrage, summoning 2 Warden Enforcers.
